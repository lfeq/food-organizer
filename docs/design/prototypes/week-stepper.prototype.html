Language ES is the fit test — "Semana siguiente" is 2× "Next week"

PROTOTYPE — What does the week stepper look like?

Issue #75 on map #31. What happens to the sidebar on a phone? settled the behaviour: Next week stops being a navigation destination at either width and becomes one affordance in the week header that flips by which week is shown. No artboard draws it, so its visual treatment is open.

Four boards below. They disagree about where the affordance lives and what kind of control it is, not about colour. Each renders in both week states, because a control whose whole job is to flip is only judgeable as a pair.

Two facts that change the frame the ticket was written in. First, Print is out of scope (ruled while resolving Which of the five week-screen variants is the direction?), so the desktop header carries one button, not the two the ticket assumed — the desktop is no longer the crowded width; the phone is, alone. Second, the green/dark rule already has a navigation exemption on the books: the tab bar's active marker is green on every cell because that rule "governs what an action acts on, and a navigation indicator acts on nothing". Stepping between two weeks moves the view without touching a plan, so green here follows precedent rather than breaking the rule — but ink follows it too, and that is the real choice.

RESOLVED. B — Text action under the title, in --accent green, at both widths. The losing boards stay below on purpose: the reasoning is only legible next to what it beat. The decision lives in docs/design/visual-system.md, not here.

Arrow-only is not among the four. Decide the palette's accessibility floor left a standing rule — a glyph that is the whole control is not decoration — and a bare → in a header would have to say "next week" with no word to lean on, beside a ← that means "this week" rather than "previous week". The arrow rides a word in every board here; only its weight varies.

Phone — 390px, the tight width

Each board twice: showing this week (the affordance points forward) and showing next week (it points back). Same header content underneath, same today card, same compact rows. Next week has no today card, which is 1e's state.

Desktop — 900px, at the breakpoint

The sidebar is four items, Next week no longer among them. With Print out of scope the header holds one green pill, so every board has room here that it does not have on the phone — which is why the phone frames above decide this and the desktop frames only have to not fight the choice.
